🎉 Don’t Assume. Investigate.

  • Review: examine the complaint and all relevant information provided.
  • Verify: check records, transactions and supporting evidence to establish the facts.
  • Engage: contact the relevant teams or departments where more information is needed.
  • Compare: check the information gathered against the customer’s complaint.
  • Recommend: decide the next steps based on the findings.

A complaint should be investigated objectively and thoroughly. The right resolution starts with understanding what went wrong.
